Ad Skipper automatically skips ads in YouTube and YouTube Music by tapping the Skip button for you. It runs quietly in the background while those apps are open.

Features:
- Auto-skip ads in YouTube and YouTube Music
- Skip timing: Off (instant) or wait 5, 10, or 15 seconds before skipping, so creators still get ad revenue
- Pause list: automatically pauses itself when banking apps, password managers, and other Accessibility-sensitive apps are in front
- Skip log: shows every skip with timestamp and button details
- Heuristic fallback: survives YouTube UI updates by finding the Skip button's position when exact matching fails

How it works:
Ad Skipper uses Android's Accessibility Service API. It watches the screen while YouTube is in the foreground, finds the ad's Skip button, and taps it.

It does not modify YouTube, does not use any network connection, and does not collect or send any data.

Requirements:
- Android 8.1 or newer
- Accessibility Service must be enabled for this app

Note: Ad Skipper cannot skip non-skippable ads. If YouTube does not show a Skip button, there is nothing for the app to tap.
